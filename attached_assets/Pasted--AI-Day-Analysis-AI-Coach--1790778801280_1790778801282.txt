لكن يجب أن نفصل داخل النظام بين شيئين:

AI Day Analysis
= ماذا يحدث في يومي؟

AI Coach
= ماذا أفعل بناءً على ما يحدث؟

02 — AI Day Analysis + AI Coach
الهدف

بعد أن ينهي المستخدم جزءًا من تتبع يومه، لا نريده أن يرى:

Social Media: 3h 42m

فقط.

نريد أن يخرج من الشاشة وهو يعرف:

ماذا يحدث؟
لماذا قد يكون مهمًا؟
ما التغيير الصغير الذي يمكنني تجربته؟
ماذا يمكنني أن أضع بدل الوقت الذي سأستعيده؟

وهنا تبدأ رحلة الـAI:

Time Data
   ↓
Calculate Facts
   ↓
Detect Patterns
   ↓
AI Interpretation
   ↓
AI Recommendation
   ↓
User Choice
   ↓
Habit Builder
1. نقطة الدخول

بعد شاشة Your Day التي بنيناها في الميزة السابقة، يظهر قسم:

✨ Your Coach

مثلاً:

I noticed something about your day.

ثم Card:

You spent 3h 42m on social media today.

Most of this happened between 8:00 PM and 11:00 PM.

ثم:

You could start by reclaiming just 20 minutes.

زر:

See what your coach suggests
2. لا نفتح Chatbot مباشرة

هذه نقطة UX مهمة.

لا أريد أن تكون التجربة:

🤖 Hello, how can I help you?

لأننا لا نبني ChatGPT آخر.

نريد أن يكون الـAI مبادرًا.

هو يقول:

"لاحظت هذا."

ثم:

"أعتقد أن هذه قد تكون فرصة جيدة."

ثم المستخدم يقرر.

3. التحليل على ثلاث طبقات
Layer 1 — Facts

هذه من التطبيق، وليست من AI.

مثلاً:

Tracked time = 11h 20m

Social Media = 222 min
Study = 130 min
Work = 95 min
Exercise = 30 min
Rest = 80 min

والتطبيق يحسب:

Social Media %
= 222 / 680 × 100
= 32.6%

الـAI لا يعيد حسابها.

4. Layer 2 — Patterns

هنا النظام يستخرج Patterns قبل إرسالها إلى AI.

مثلاً:

Top category = Social Media

Peak period = 20:00–23:00

Repeated blocks = 4

Average session length = 18 min

Previous days:
Day 1 = 1h 40m
Day 2 = 2h 15m
Day 3 = 3h 05m
Day 4 = 3h 42m

ثم نرسل هذه facts إلى الـAI.

5. Layer 3 — AI Interpretation

هنا يبدأ دور Coach:

"Your social media use is concentrated in the evening, especially after you've finished your main responsibilities."

ثم:

"Instead of trying to eliminate it, you could experiment with reclaiming 20 minutes during that window."

لاحظي أننا لا نقول:

"Social media is bad."

نقول:

"هذه فرصة يمكن تجربتها."

6. أسلوب الـAI

نريد الشخصية:

Smart

يفهم البيانات.

Human

يتحدث بشكل طبيعي.

Direct

يمكن أن يكون جريئًا عندما تكون البيانات واضحة.

Supportive

بدون لوم.

Action-oriented

لا يترك المستخدم مع Insight فقط.

مثال جيد:

You spent 3h 42m on social media today — mostly between 8 PM and 11 PM.

You don't need to change your entire routine.

Let's try taking back 20 minutes from that window.

7. مستوى الجرأة

بما أنك وافقتِ أن يكون الـAI أكثر جرأة، يمكن أن نستخدم مستويات:

Gentle

You might want to explore...

Direct

You are spending more time here than you planned.

Strong

This is currently your biggest time opportunity today.

لكن لا نستخدم لغة:

You're wasting your life.

أو:

You have no self-control.

9. AI لا يفرض Replacement

مثلاً المستخدم يختار:

Reading

لكن لا نعتبر أن:

Reading = 20 min

أصبحت عادة نهائية.

ننتقل إلى Habit Builder.

10. AI Habit Builder

المستخدم:

أريد القراءة ساعة يوميًا.

الـAI يفهم:

Desired outcome = Reading
Desired duration = 60 min

ويقول:

ساعة يوميًا قد تكون بداية ثقيلة.

أقترح أن تبدأ بـ10 دقائق.

ثم:

Your first step

Read for 10 minutes

Minimum

5 minutes

وهنا الفرق مهم:

Target ≠ Minimum

مثلاً:

Target = 10 min
Minimum = 5 min

إذا كان اليوم صعبًا جدًا، يستطيع المستخدم تنفيذ 5 دقائق وما زال يعتبر اليوم ناجحًا.

11. كيف AI يقرر أن الهدف كبير؟

في V1 لا نحتاج Model يتعلم ذلك.

نضع قواعد.

مثلاً:

IF requested_duration >= 60 min
AND habit is new
THEN recommend smaller start

IF frequency = daily
AND target is high
THEN recommend lower minimum

IF user says "I haven't done this before"
THEN start very small

ثم AI يصيغ الاقتراح.

16. Post-check-in Coach

بعد إكمال العادة:

المستخدم يضغط:

✅ Done

تظهر:

How did it feel?

😊 Easy
😐 Normal
😣 Hard

ثم AI يتدخل فقط إذا كان هناك شيء مفيد يقوله.

مثلاً:

أو:

"You completed your minimum. That's enough for today."

17. إذا كان Hard

مثلاً:

😣 Hard

AI:

That's useful to know.

ثم:

Tomorrow we'll keep the minimum at 5 minutes instead of increasing the target.

18. إذا كان Very Hard

هنا لا ننتظر 3 أيام بالضرورة.

يمكن أن يكون لدينا نظام:

Very Hard once
→ no automatic drastic change

Very Hard twice
→ flag for adaptation

Very Hard 3 times
→ reduce target

بهذا لا يتفاعل النظام بشكل مبالغ فيه مع يوم سيئ واحد.

19. إذا كان Easy

إذا:

Easy
Easy
Easy
Easy

يمكن أن يقترح:

You've been handling this comfortably.

Would you like to increase your target from 10 → 15 minutes?

مع:

Yes

Keep it

دائمًا المستخدم يقرر.

20. Failure Coach

😫 Too difficult
🕐 No time
😴 Forgot
😕 Lost motivation
⚡ Something unexpected
✍️ Other

المستخدم يختار.

21. AI يفسر سبب الانتكاسة
Too difficult

Your minimum may still be too large for your current routine.

No time

You may need a smaller version for busy days.

Forgot

Your habit may need a stronger cue.

Lost motivation

Your current routine may rely too much on motivation. Let's make the action easier to start.

ثم يقترح التعديل.

22. AI يستطيع تغيير "شكل" العادة

ليس فقط الرقم.

Forgot.

مثلاً:

المستخدم لا يحتاج معرفة المصطلح.

إذا كان الوقت مناسبًا:

24. AI Daily Insight لا يجب أن يظهر كل يوم بنفس الشكل

Consistency

Adaptation

Encouragement

You kept going even on a hard day.

37. Prompt كامل لـReplit

وهذا هو الجزء الذي يمكنك نسخه مباشرة إلى Replit Agent:

Implement Feature 02: AI Day Analysis + AI Coach.

IMPORTANT:
Do not redesign unrelated features.
Do not replace the existing authentication/database architecture.
Do not break the current time tracking implementation.
Inspect the existing project first and reuse the current services/components/routes.

PRODUCT GOAL:

The AI should not behave like a generic chatbot.

It should:
1. Understand the user's tracked day.
2. Identify meaningful patterns.
3. Suggest one realistic small change.
4. Suggest replacement activities for reclaimed time.
5. Help the user turn a large goal into a small habit.
6. Adapt the habit based on completion and difficulty.
7. Learn from missed-day reasons.
8. Communicate naturally in Arabic.
9. Always allow the user to accept, reject, or edit AI suggestions.

IMPORTANT ARCHITECTURE:

The application is responsible for:
- calculating time totals
- percentages
- category totals
- consistency
- day numbers
- coin calculations
- reward unlocks
- target calculations
- deterministic adaptation rules

The AI is responsible for:
- interpretation
- pattern explanation
- recommendations
- replacement suggestions
- habit decomposition
- coaching language
- failure feedback

Do not let the LLM become the source of truth for numeric application state.

AI FLOW:

A) DAY ANALYSIS

Input:
- total tracked minutes
- category totals
- category percentages
- peak time blocks
- previous-day comparison if available
- current habits
- user interests if available

Output JSON:

{
  "type": "pattern | progress | opportunity | consistency | no_major_change",
  "headline": "...",
  "observation": "...",
  "pattern": "...",
  "suggested_change": {
    "minutes": 20,
    "category": "social_media"
  },
  "replacement_options": [
    {
      "title": "...",
      "minutes": 20,
      "reason": "..."
    }
  ],
  "coach_message": "..."
}

B) HABIT BUILDER

When the user enters a large goal such as:
"I want to read for 1 hour every day."

Return:

{
  "habit_name": "Reading",
  "target": 10,
  "minimum": 5,
  "unit": "minutes",
  "frequency": "daily",
  "reason": "...",
  "coach_message": "..."
}

The AI should be allowed to recommend a smaller starting action.

Never force the user to accept the recommendation.

C) ADAPTATION

Use deterministic rules first.

Example:
- repeated Hard/Very Hard ratings -> reduce target
- repeated Easy ratings -> suggest small increase
- missed day -> ask why
- Too difficult -> reduce next minimum
- No time -> offer smaller version
- Forgot -> suggest cue/reminder adjustment
- Lost motivation -> reduce friction / simplify

AI only explains the adjustment naturally.

D) FAILURE COACH

Input:
- missed-day reason
- recent completion history
- difficulty history
- current target/minimum

Return:
{
  "interpretation": "...",
  "next_step": "...",
  "recommended_adjustment": "...",
  "coach_message": "..."
}

UX:

1. Dashboard shows a "Your Coach" section after enough data exists.
2. Do not open a generic chatbot.
3. AI should proactively surface one meaningful insight.
4. Show a concise insight card.
5. Allow:
   - Accept
   - Not now
   - Choose another replacement
   - Create my own habit
6. If user selects a replacement, continue directly to habit creation.
7. Show target and minimum separately.
8. After habit completion, show difficulty options:
   - Easy
   - Normal
   - Hard
   - Very hard
9. After missed day, show reason options:
   - Too difficult
   - No time
   - Forgot
   - Lost motivation
   - Unexpected event
   - Other
10. Use failure information for later adaptation.

LANGUAGE:

The application is Arabic-first and RTL.

All user-facing AI messages must be in natural Arabic.

Avoid:
- shame
- guilt
- diagnosis
- medical claims
- exaggerated claims
- statements that imply 22 days scientifically creates a habit

Use supportive but direct language.

AI SAFETY/QUALITY:

- Never invent user behavior.
- Never invent time data.
- Do not infer private information not present in provided context.
- If data is insufficient, say so.
- Do not expose API keys.
- Validate structured AI output.
- If AI fails, use deterministic fallback messages.

PERFORMANCE:

- Do not call the AI API on every page load.
- Cache day analysis when the underlying data has not changed.
- Only call the AI when meaningful new information exists.
- Keep AI responses concise.

LOADING STATES:

Use:
"نراجع يومك..."
"نبحث عن نمط واضح..."
"نجهز لك خطوة صغيرة..."

ERROR STATE:

Do not expose technical errors.

Show:
"مدربك يحتاج لحظة، لكن ما زال بإمكاننا الاستمرار."

Then use deterministic fallback.

IMPLEMENT:

1. Inspect current AI/backend implementation.
2. Inspect existing time tracking and habit APIs.
3. Reuse current data structures.
4. Add the AI service if missing.
5. Add validation for AI JSON.
6. Add dashboard coach UI.
7. Add habit-builder AI UI.
8. Add failure-coach flow.
9. Connect AI results to the existing habit/journey system.
10. Test real Gemini API requests.
11. Test AI failure fallback.
12. Test insufficient-data state.
13. Test Arabic RTL rendering.
14. Do not implement new unrelated features.

Before editing, provide a short list of the current files/services that will be changed.
